# SCHOLA-FORGE Claim Lock

**Thesis:** The system produces bounded explainable instructional-routing decisions with explicit rationale and teacher override under declared software scenarios.

**Evidence scope:** internal and adversarial software tests.

**Excluded claims:** durable learning, universal benefit, equity impact, teacher-burden reduction, classroom effectiveness.

**Primary falsifier:** a declared scenario where the explanation is inconsistent with the routing decision or a required override is unavailable.

**Next evidence action:** bounded design-paper evaluation or governed delayed retention/transfer study.
