# ScholaForge — Technical Description

## Field of the Invention

The present invention relates to AI-powered education platforms, specifically
to systems and methods for providing equitable, explainable, and universally
beneficial learning experiences.

## Background

Traditional education systems face challenges in personalization, accessibility,
equity, and explainability. Existing AI-based education tools often lack
transparency in their decision-making processes.

## Summary

ScholaForge is a unified education platform that combines adaptive learning,
explainable AI, and universal benefit principles to create an education system
that serves all learners equitably.
